About Us

At Roast & Grain, we believe in slow roasting and natural fermentation to deliver unparalleled flavor in every cup and bite.

Founded in 2018, our coffee house pairs sustainably sourced beans with small-batch pastries crafted fresh every single morning.

Our Specialties

Hand-crafted espresso beverages, poured with care by our certified baristas using seasonal single-origin roasts.

Flaky, butter croissants and sourdough pastries baked fresh daily using organic, locally sourced flour.

Precision pour-over bar highlighting nuanced floral, fruity, and chocolate notes in our micro-lot origins.

Naturally leavened sourdough loaves with a crisp crust and open crumb, available warm every afternoon.

Single Origin Brews

Distinctive tasting profiles sourced directly from ethical farming co-ops across Ethiopia, Colombia, and Guatemala.

Artisan Bakery

Handcrafted breads, cinnamon buns, and fruit tarts prepared daily without artificial additives.

Cold Brew & Teas

Slow-steeped 24-hour nitro cold brews paired alongside organic loose-leaf herbal teas.
